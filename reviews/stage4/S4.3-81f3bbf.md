## Review
**Standards.** One place handles retained generations, with a clear module comment on the representation. Reset clears every store. Validation covers each reference.

There is one low, non-blocking note. `share_imported` interns an imported record under the digest the export names (`_intern_record(records[rd], rd)`) and does not recompute it. A hand-edited export that keys a record under another record's digest would be accepted. Unchanged exports, which are what the spec requires the service to accept, are unaffected. Recomputing the digest on import would close this.

**Spec.** The L10/L12 semantics hold: round trip, merge, imported token wins, and reset clears all. The export now grows with distinct ledger content.
