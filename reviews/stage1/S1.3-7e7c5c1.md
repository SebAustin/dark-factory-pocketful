## Findings

**F2 — §8 GET /activity ordering (blocking).** Spec: "Payments visible to the caller by the
feed contract in §4, newest first by `created_at`." §3.4: "Timestamps in responses are RFC 3339
with an explicit offset, e.g. `2026-09-24T19:00:00+02:00`."
Observed: `newest_first` sorts the `created_at` strings, and `_opt_ts` keeps the fixture's own
offset. A seeded payment at `19:00:00+02:00` (17:00Z) is listed before one at
`18:30:00+00:00` (18:30Z).
Expected: p_late (18:30Z) first.
Fix direction: normalise the timestamps to one offset when loading, or sort on the parsed instant.
Reproduce (fresh container):
```sh
curl -s -X POST localhost:18403/_test/reset -d '{"currency":"EUR","minor_units":2,
 "users":[{"id":"u_a","email":"a@e.com","password":"correct horse","display_name":"a","handle":"a","balance":100},
```
