## Findings

**F3 — §10 (blocking).** Spec: "It must accept an unchanged export produced by this service."
Observed: after any split that produces a zero share (legal per §9: "A share of `0` is legal and still produces a request for that participant"), `GET /_test/export` followed by `POST /_test/import` of that body → 422 validation_failed.
Cause: `_check_requests` requires `amount` from 1 to 2^53.
Expected: 204.
Reproduce:
```sh

curl -s -X POST localhost:18404/splits -H "Authorization: Bearer $T" -H 'Idempotency-Key: z' \
  -d '{"amount":1,"participant_handles":["ada","bob","cy"]}'
curl -s localhost:18404/_test/export > exp.json
curl -s -i -X POST localhost:18404/_test/import --data-binary @exp.json   # 422, expected 204
```
