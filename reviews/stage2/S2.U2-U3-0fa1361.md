## Findings

**F3 — Spec: "The required flows must remain clear and usable at a 375 CSS-pixel viewport and at conventional desktop widths,
without horizontal page scrolling."** The signed-in header (from U1) does not fit between the 641 px breakpoint, where the desktop nav appears,
and about 920 px with ordinary data, and up to about 1030 px with a long name or balance:
- Fixture "Ada Lovelace" / `ada` / 100.00 EUR: `scrollWidth` 917 at **768** and 917 at **900** (the page scrolls sideways; Log out is off-screen).
- Plan fixture (long name, 20-character handle, 12-digit balance): `scrollWidth` 1029 at **768** and at **1024**. At 1024 the Log out
  button is drawn **on top of** the user name (`shots/U3-0fa1361-mid/1024/01-shell-requests.png`). At 1280 the handle runs 5 px under Log out.
Repro: `$PY reviews/stage2/tools/header_overlap.py http://127.0.0.1:<port>` (prints the overlap per width), or
`walker.py --plan reviews/stage2/tools/plan.u3.json --viewports 768,1024` → `overflow`.
Expected: no horizontal scroll and no overlap at any width from 375 up (e.g. keep the tab bar until the full nav fits, let the balance
chip yield first, and cap or wrap the user chip).
